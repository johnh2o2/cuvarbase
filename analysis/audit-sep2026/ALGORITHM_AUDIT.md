# cuvarbase algorithm soundness and performance audit

Date: 2026-09-04. Tree: branch `v1.0-fixes`, HEAD `7d55ea2` (md5-verified identical between the local repo and the `/workspace/cuvarbase` editable install on the RTX 4090 pod for every file cited below). Nothing in the tree was modified; all fixes below were validated only through scratch copies and in-process monkeypatches.

How this report was produced. Ten auditors (bls-core, bls-sparse-batch, tls, lombscargle-nfft, pdm-ce, nufft-lrt, cross-cutting, perf-bls, perf-tls-ls, perf-pdm-ce-lrt-general) ran independent reference comparisons on the pod and filed 163 findings (7 blocker, 55 high, 58 medium, 43 low; 155 of 163 with a device experiment). The 26 blocker/high correctness topics (37 finding ids) were then re-reproduced by independent verifiers with their own scripts: 25 confirmed, 1 downgraded (`lrt-upper-half-band`, high to medium), 0 refuted. The 25 high-severity performance findings and all 101 medium/low findings were NOT independently re-run; they are labelled "auditor-measured, unverified" throughout. Timings are from a shared 4090 inside a CPU-throttled container and are relative comparisons only (the archived benchmarks are A5000).

Status vocabulary used below: CONFIRMED = reproduced on device by a verifier from the tree at HEAD; DOWNGRADED = defect real, severity claim not supported; UNVERIFIED = auditor evidence only. Severity rubric: blocker = wrong science on a default path with valid input; high = wrong or undocumented behaviour a normal user can hit, or a misleading statistic; medium = edge case, precision, misleading docstring; low = hygiene.

Headline: the core statistics (BLS, sparse BLS, TLS, GLS, PDM, CE, matched-filter/Detector A algebra) are implemented correctly where the auditors could compare against references (agreement 1e-8 to 1e-14), but seven distinct default-path defects give wrong science with valid input (BLS uint32 overflow and bin-buffer overrun, TLS fixed duration window, two Lomb-Scargle NFFT defects, NUFFT-LRT float32 BJD cast and single-phase default), and 18 further confirmed high-severity defects sit on default or documented paths. All 25 confirmed fixes are trivial-to-medium effort; 11 of them (defects 2-11 and 24 below) change numbers or keys returned by default calls and therefore need the GPU gate re-run and a CHANGELOG line, defect 1 changes only calls that today overflow or crash, and defect 22 changes the marginal detector's own default.

BLS core. The weighted BLS statistic is exactly 1 - chi2/chi2_0 of Kovacs et al. with normalized 1/dy^2 weights; the `snr` and `loglik` conversions equal astropy's `objective='snr'` (14.27136 vs 14.27136) and likelihood x (1 - r) to all printed digits; the fused/optimized/standard fast kernels match a float64 replica to |dP| <= 3e-8 for five (qmin, qmax, dlogq, noverlap, dphi) settings, at t = 0 and at BJD 2.46e6; the compiled float32 fold is bit-identical to numpy; wrapped-boundary transits, time order, duplicates and dy rescaling are all handled. The default `eebls_transit` path for ndata >= 500 (`eebls_gpu`) is the problem: (a) `bin_and_phase_fold_bst_multifreq` bounds its thread index with a 32-bit `ndata * nfreq` and the host never caps the auto batch, so a bare `eebls_transit(t, y, dy)` on a 262,800-point 2-min-cadence year (4.9e9 threads on the 24 GB card) returns 65,372 zeros out of 87,835 powers and misses the injected planet (peak at 7.40 d instead of 3.70 d; corr 0.34 with a safe batch size); (b) device bin buffers are sized from `count_tot_nbins(global nbins0_max, nbinsf_max)`, which is not an upper bound over batches (non-monotone in nbins0: 1875, 1939, 1704 for nb0 = 28, 29, 30 at nbinsf = 359), so `eebls_transit(t, y, dy, fmin=0.02, fmax=0.5)` on 70,000 points dies with `cuMemcpyDtoH failed: an illegal memory access` at the card's default memory budget (14 of 1155 scanned default-path configurations overrun); (c) per-frequency Keplerian q bounds are collapsed to one (min, max) pair per batch, so 31,966 of 36,585 default `eebls_transit` solutions lie outside their own window, the off-peak noise floor is 1.5x higher than the bound-honouring fast kernel, ~10x of binning work is wasted, and the periodogram depends on `freq_batch_size` and therefore on free GPU memory (28,628 of 36,585 frequencies differ by > 1e-4 between a 24 GB and a simulated 7 GB card). Unverified auditor items add that the fast path never tests qmax and loses 15-50% of exact power for boxes near qmin (id 64), and that the float32 fold loses 3-22% for q ~ 0.01 boxes at T*f > 7000 (ids 65, 158).

Sparse BLS and batch BLS. Sparse BLS is algorithmically correct on centered data (all N(N-1)+1 cyclic runs enumerated, wrap-around included, q filter honoured; 5e-8..1e-6 vs an independent reference), and `eebls_gpu_batch` reproduces `eebls_gpu_fast` to <= 6e-8 across ndata 63..20,000, noverlap 1..8, ragged and chunked batches with per-frequency q bounds in all three conventions. But both GPU sparse kernels accumulate float32 prefix sums of uncentered w*y (`sparse_bls.cu:214-217`) and only later subtract ybar*W, and the wrapper casts y to float32 uncentered (`bls.py:2080`) unlike the dense path, so on ordinary mag-12 / 5-mmag data (the default `eebls_transit` path for ndata < 500) powers are off by 0.1-3% across the grid and 1.7% at the peak, the argmax jitters by 1-3 grid steps (<= 0.75 peak widths), and one point 1000x more precise than the rest gives power 52..306 (> 1) at every frequency with a wrong argmax. The opt-in `use_simple` kernel still carries `MAX_W_COMPLEMENT 1E-9` (untouched by 2cc1f96): 30 of 40 single-site seeds return power up to 4.64 in pure noise. Unverified: reported q biased low by ~1/(2N) (id 74), no minimum in-transit points (id 78), sort-order dependence at float32 phase ties (id 73), `noverlap=0` returns zeros on the batch path (id 75).

TLS. Both the exact legacy kernel and the default binned batch kernel with top-K refinement are sound for what they compute: on 7 batman injections (P = 1.3-25 d, rp = 0.02-0.10, b = 0/0.85) run on the reference's own period grid the period is within one grid step 7/7, depth within 2-5%, SDE within 5-10% in the SDE 15-30 regime, and the 400-LC null SDE distribution (6.43 +/- 1.00) matches the reference (6.54 +/- 1.14). Three defects: the no-qmin/qmax default searches a fixed q window [0.005, 0.15] at every period while the Ofir grid runs to span/2, so beyond P ~ 60 d (Sun-like; 18.5 d for an M dwarf) no trial duration is physical and a 365-d transit on a 1400-d light curve comes back at 182.5 d with half the depth and SDE 66.9; the `FAP` key is a fixed piecewise function of SDE (discontinuous at SDE = 7: 0.100 to 0.010) unrelated to the null, which itself moves with grid and baseline (4%, 9%, 23%, 92% of pure noise above SDE 7 for four configurations); and `T0` is a phase relative to floor(min t) on the fast path, a phase relative to t = 0 on the legacy path, and an absolute time (which can precede the first observation) on the batch path. Unverified medium items: no free baseline term (id 80), SR = 1 - chi2/max(chi2) rather than the reference's chi2_min/chi2 (2x SDE difference for strong signals; ids 81, 146), medfilt zero-padding edge bias (id 83), coarse t0 grid losses (id 84), unsorted period grids corrupt SDE/uncertainty (id 82).

Lomb-Scargle, NFFT, multiharmonic GLS, FAP. The GLS formulas, the CPU multiharmonic direct sums with ridge regularization, and `fap_baluev` all match independent references to float64 roundoff (3e-14, 5e-16, 1e-14). The default device path does not: the w-spectrum NFFT is gridded with the psi tables of the yw grid although the grids differ in size (`lombscargle_memory.py:186-190`), which displaces every point's Gaussian by frac(ng_yw*x) - frac(ng_w*x) cells (max|S_w - exact| 1.2e-1 vs 7e-8 for the yw grid) and biases power by 3e-3..2.4e-2 on every call in float32 and float64; the NFFT grids are sized sigma*nf while modes k0..k0+nf-1 are read (`lombscargle_memory.py:193-194`), so any grid with fmin >= fmax/2 (e.g. `run(minimum_frequency=20, maximum_frequency=30)`) reads aliased modes and returns powers of 1e4..1e36 with a wrong best frequency and `significance = 1.0`; and `floorf` on a double coordinate (`cunfft.cu:151`) misplaces ~n0*ng/2^24 points by one cell, making `use_double=True` less accurate than float32 (4.2e-2 vs 1.0e-2 rel. NFFT error; LS 1.46e-2 vs 1.2e-3 vs astropy at survey scale). Three further confirmed defects are off the default path: `nharmonics > 1` with `use_fft=False` (or `python_dir_sums=True`) returns the H = 1 periodogram; `amplitude_prior` is ignored when `nharmonics > 1`; and `check_k0` inspects only `freqs[0:2]`, so a non-uniform grid is silently evaluated on the implied uniform grid and returned under the user's labels (corr 0.009 with astropy at the user's frequencies). The public `NFFTAsyncProcess` returns wrong |ghat| for absolute times (rel 0.94 at t + 2457000.5) because it stores float32 absolute t. Unverified medium items: `significance = 1 - FAP` saturates at 1.0 (ids 96, 129, 144), cufinufft rejects `use_double` (id 97), unreduced float32 phases give 0.4-1.3% peak error on dense high-frequency grids (id 160).

PDM. All four dispersion statistics (binned step, binned linear interpolation, binless tophat, binless Gaussian) and their `_fast` variants agree with float64 re-implementations to the level of float32 bin-edge flips (5e-4..3.6e-2; 4.4e-7 against a float32-phase reference), `_fast` equals original to <= 1.2e-6, batch/multi-LC/per-LC-grid paths are bit-identical to single runs, and the deprecated (t, y, w, freqs) path equals the modern path exactly when w sums to one. No blocker or high finding. Auditor-measured caveats: the returned value is 1 - SS_within/SS_tot with no degrees-of-freedom correction (pure-noise mean (M - 1)/(N - 1) = 0.40 at N = 20 while the docs and notebook quote Stellingwerf's formulas and "Theta ~ 1 for noise"; id 110); folding is float32-only with no double option, so at 3650 d x 50 c/d x 50 bins the argmax moves (1056 to 759; id 109); unnormalized w on the deprecated path returns a flat 1.0 spectrum (id 111); `binned_step` reads `bin_means[NBINS]` when a phase rounds to exactly 1.0 (id 113); the `_fast` kernels are not faster on Ada (0.7-2.0x; ids 114, 159).

Conditional entropy. The count histogram and H = sum N log(dm Nphi/N) / sum N match a numpy Graham et al. implementation to ~1e-7 only when the reference reproduces two real defects. `setdata` normalizes y to [0, 1] and takes floor(y*mag_bins), so the brightest point gets index mag_bins on every run; no kernel clamps it, so it lands in bin (n+1, 0), in the next frequency's (0, 0) bin, or 4 bytes past `bins_g` (guard-element proof), and in shared memory it lands on `block_bin_phi[0]`. Consequences: every unweighted spectrum is biased O(1/N) (7e-3 at N = 500, 6e-2 at N = 60, 0.45 at N = 5), the standard kernel's output depends on the order of the frequency grid (4.6e-3 at N = 500), and standard and fast kernels disagree (4.6e-3; wildly at N = 3). Weighted CE skips a magnitude bin using only the distance to its lower edge (`ce.cu:68-69`): 39% of points lose > 5% of their Gaussian mass, the brightest point loses 100%, spectra shift by up to 8.6e-2 nat and the argmin by one grid step. `use_double=True` with `use_fast=True` crashes the CUDA context whenever (mag_bins + 1) * phase_bins is odd (byte remainder used as an element offset). `balanced_magbins` and `widen_mag_range` passed to the constructor are dropped, so the test parametrization exercises the plain kernel and weighted+balanced does not raise as documented. `preallocate()` never uploads the frequency grid (every frequency evaluated at f = 0, constant output) and leaves `memory.stream = None`, so results read after `finish()` are stale in 25-29 of 30 runs; the same stream defect gives `LombScargleAsyncProcess.preallocate` stale reads in 14 of 30 runs. Unverified: module recompiled on every `run()` (124-132 ms per call, id 59), fast-path grid of 5 blocks on a 128-SM GPU (id 61), option-combination silences (id 105), zero-width balanced bins give -inf (id 106), `set_data=False` accumulates histograms (id 112).

NUFFT-LRT including Detector A and sequential. The matched-filter core Re sum Y T*/P / sqrt(sum |T|^2/P) is implemented as documented, Detector A's Woodbury form reproduces a dense real-space Cov_z^-1 to 1.5e-16 with the correct prior-to-0 and prior-to-infinity limits, and it tracks the exact time-domain Detector A as a constant ~1.5x multiple across prior widths 0.03-10 sigma. The default path is nevertheless wrong science: `run()` casts t to float32 before folding the box template and gridding (0.25-d spacing at BJD 2457000), so BJD-scale input changes the periodogram to corr 0.48-0.50 on all three detectors and moves the sequential detector's best period from 4.4 to 6.4 d (use_double is invariant to 5e-9); `epochs=None` evaluates one phase-0 template per (P, dur), so it recovers a randomly placed transit 0 times in 12 (48-epoch grid: 12/12) while the class docstring, README example 1 and the shipped example present it as a period search (the example's printed "detection" at 3.59 d has SNR 0.64 at the true cell); `_sequential_detrend` is OLS without intercept on un-centred y and V, so a 1% column mean leaves a 1% residual systematic (SNR@P 25.0 to 5.4, wrong period); and Detector A with the default `estimate_psd=True` estimates the PSD from y - V mu, which still contains V(c - mu) spread over the whole band by the spectral window (median PSD inflation 36x), whitening away the transit (completeness 0.13 vs 0.47 at depth 0.008 with a residual-based PSD, exactly the campaign's lrt_marg < lrt_seq pattern). The default sigma = 2 makes modes k >= nf/2 aliased at O(1) (algorithmic, also in double; non-deterministic in float32), but the verifier showed this changes z-scores by < 2% and no ranking, so it was downgraded to medium. Separately, the null standard deviation of the statistic with the TRUE white PSD is 0.97/1.38/1.95 (uniform, nf = n/2n/4n) and 2.16-2.65 (ground sampling), reproduced by an exact DFT: the "SNR" is not N(0, 1) by construction, which the README misattributes to PSD estimation.

Cross-cutting input handling. Every default path is BJD-safe (t + 2457000.5 and t + 2460000.0 match t ~ 0..100 to <= 1e-6, mostly bit-exact) except `nufft_lrt` and `NFFTAsyncProcess`; results are invariant to input order, duplicated times and dy rescaling; batch equals single to ~1e-7; run-to-run differences are ~1e-8 (float atomics). There is no input validator on any GPU entry point except `sparse_bls_cpu/gpu` and `tls_search_batch`: NaN/inf/dy = 0 give finite wrong periodograms (rel ~1, wrong argmax) on the BLS fast/standard/batch and CE paths, all-NaN on PDM, and a -1 sentinel at every frequency on the LS NFFT path (`lomb.cu:86-87`, documented nowhere); NaN per-frequency q bounds or `eebls_transit_gpu(use_fast=True)` with N <= 4 or a NaN timestamp raise `cuMemcpyDtoH failed: an illegal memory access` and every later GPU call in the process fails (`ensure_context()` returns the cached module). The default `eebls_transit` with N <= 4 raises a ValueError (so the context-death claim is `use_fast=True` only). Unverified medium items: worker threads cannot launch kernels (id 124), poisoned-context errors are not translated (id 125), binned results move up to 10% (BLS) / 2% (TLS) with the fractional part of min(t) (id 130), float32 absolute-scale times silently destroy every method (id 131).

## 2. Defects to fix before 1.0

Ordered most severe first: CONFIRMED blockers, CONFIRMED highs, the one DOWNGRADED topic, then unverified medium/low auditor findings worth fixing. "Default-path change: yes" means the fix alters numbers or keys returned by a default call on valid input and therefore needs the GPU gate re-run plus a CHANGELOG line.

### 2.1 Confirmed blockers

**1. `bls-overflow-oob` [blocker, CONFIRMED on device] (ids 1, 38).** Two bundled defects on the `eebls_transit` default path for ndata >= 500 (`eebls_gpu`). (a) Thread index and bound are 32-bit (`bls_common.cuh:12-14` `get_id()` returns `unsigned int`; `:304` `if (i < ndata * nfreq)`; same at `:349` for `bin_and_phase_fold_custom`) while the host launches the exact Python product (`bls.py:1581`, `:1320`) and never caps the auto batch (`bls.py:1504`, `:1247`). Evidence: `eebls_transit(t, y, dy)` with no kwargs on 262,800 points at 2-min cadence auto-sizes `freq_batch_size=18,551` on the 24 GB card (4.9e9 > 2^32): 65,372 of 87,835 powers exactly 0, peak at P = 7.3997 d vs 3.6999 d with `freq_batch_size=8000`, corr 0.34; auditor case 66,000 x 66,000: max power 1.678 (> 1 impossible), 65,075 zeros, corr -0.003. Default Kepler long-cadence (65-70K points) does NOT overflow on a 24 GB card (fbs 7503); TESS 2-min year and Kepler short cadence do. (b) Bin buffers sized from `count_tot_nbins(nbins0_max, nbinsf_max)` (`bls.py:1486-1489`, `:1509`), non-monotone in nbins0 (`bls.py:1380-1387`: nb0 = 28/29/30 gives 1875/1939/1704 at nbinsf = 359), per-batch `nbins_tot` at `:1558-1562` can exceed it, and `all_bins` (`:1594`) is never checked. Evidence: `eebls_transit(t, y, dy, fmin=0.02, fmax=0.5)`, 70,000 points, default memory: batch 0 overruns by 44.5 MB and dies with `cuMemcpyDtoH failed: an illegal memory access`; compute-sanitizer: `Invalid __global__ atomic ... out of bounds`; 14 of 1155 scanned (grid, memory) default-path configurations overrun. Fix: 64-bit index and bound in both kernels (`size_t i = (size_t)blockIdx.x * blockDim.x + threadIdx.x; if (i < (size_t)ndata * nfreq)`), host cap `freq_batch_size = max(1, min(fbs, (2**31 - 1) // ndata))` at `:1504` and `:1247` (also for user-supplied values); build the batch table (imin, imax, nbins0, nbinsf, nbins_tot) before allocating and size `gs = freq_batch_size * max_over_batches(nbins_tot) * noverlap`, assert `all_bins <= gs` before every launch (keeping `freq_batch_size` as computed leaves batch boundaries and results untouched, memory grows <= ~7%). Effort: small. Default-path change: none for calls that do not overflow/overrun (bitwise modulo atomic order); overflowing calls go from garbage to correct, overrunning calls from crash to correct. Kernel edit: GPU gate re-run + CHANGELOG. Tests to add: ndata*nf > 2^32 with qmin = 0.2/qmax = 0.5 (runs ~1 s), and a Keplerian-q batched grid where batch-0 nbins0 > global nbins0_max.

**2. `tls-duration-window` [blocker, CONFIRMED] (id 9).** `tls_search_gpu`/`tls_search` without qmin/qmax use `qmin_arr = 0.005, qmax_arr = 0.15` at every period (`tls.py:596-597`; legacy kernel `tls.cu:390-392` hard-codes the same) while the default Ofir grid runs to span/2 (`tls_grids.py:127-128`). q_kep = 0.005 at P = 59.6 d (Sun-like, 1 R_earth), 18.5 d (R = M = 0.3), 99.7 d (R = 1.5, M = 1.2). Evidence (true default path, no `periods` argument, 178,532-period grid, 1400-d baseline, 30-min cadence, sigma = 3e-4, rp = 0.04): P = 365 d returns P = 182.507 d, SDE 66.9, depth 0.00092 (true 0.00198), duration 0.808 (true 0.562); `tls_transit`/`tls_search_batch` with the same R_star/M_star return 364.995 d, depth 0.00200. Degradation is graded: P = 90 d depth -6%, duration +14%; P = 180 d depth -36%, SDE 82 vs 112; P = 250 d depth -48%; M dwarf at P = 30 d on a 200-d baseline depth -8%. `R_star`/`M_star` are validated by `tls_search_gpu` (`tls.py:542`) but only feed the period grid. Fix: derive the window per period from the stellar parameters the function already takes, as `tls_search_batch` does by default (`tls.py:1291-1298`: `q = tls_grids.q_transit(periods, R_star, M_star, R_planet); qmin_arr = qmin_fac*q; qmax_arr = qmax_fac*q`, or the reference's wider T14(0.13, 0.1) .. min(T14(3.5, 1.0), 0.12) bounds), keep [0.005, 0.15] as an explicit opt-in with a warning when q_kep falls outside it, and route the legacy path through the existing 'keplerian' kernel. Effort: small. Default-path change: yes (P = 45 d: SDE 109.5 to 103.8, depth 0.00111 to 0.00109; P = 365 d: 182.5 to 365.0 d). ### 2.5 Refuted or overstated claims (do not re-chase)

- Bin-buffer overrun "silently corrupts results" (id 38): not observed; 0.44 MB and 19.5 MB overruns returned without error and matched a padded-allocation control to 1e-6. Undefined behaviour, but treat silent corruption as unverified. The crash itself is real.
- Overflow/OOB "reachability needs a narrow max_memory" (id 38's own framing): wrong in the other direction; it happens at the card's default budget with documented kwargs. Also, the default Keplerian grid for a 65-70K-point Kepler long-cadence LC does NOT overflow on a 24 GB card; the overflow needs ~250K+ points.
- `sparse_bls_simple` "returns non-finite" (id 5): 0/40 seeds non-finite; all bad outputs are finite but > 1 (up to 4.64). The ">300 power / 317.1" evidence belongs to the uncentered-float32 defect (the full kernel gives 306 on the same data; a 1E-4 patch leaves 172.7). The one-line repro with seed 0 does not trigger the bug. "compiles to W > 1.f" is imprecise (it is W > 0.999999999 in double).
- Sparse uncentered "one-line in-kernel fix, bit-neutral for centered input" (id 6): the in-kernel-only centring leaves 2e-4..1.3e-3 error and power 3.5 at R = 1e6; the wrapper must centre in float64. Bit-neutrality is 1.9e-8, not exact.
- psi-table "Baluev FAP changes by e^55" (id 12): arithmetically true for that 0.83 to 0.81 peak but irrelevant to significance; the meaningful impact is e^1..e^10 near detection thresholds.
- "0.3-2% LS error floor caused by sigma=4 / the float32 NFFT" and "document the residual ~1e-3 float32 error" (id 32): the floor is the psi-table bug (identical in double, m-independent); after the fix float32 reaches 4e-6 on the k0 = 1 grid. A sigma_eff-only fix leaves ~1e-2 on fmin >= fmax/2 grids.
- NFFT "wrong magnitudes come from float32 phase factors on un-reduced 2 pi t0 k" (id 31): a unit-modulus factor cannot change |ghat| (measured |cos + i sin| = 1.000000 at 4e7 rad); the magnitudes are wrong because `nfft_memory.py:246` casts absolute t to float32 (0.25-d spacing). The phase-factor argument holds for phases.
- NUFFT-LRT "statistic scales as sqrt(nf/n) because of the upper band double-count" and "weights[nf//2:] = 0 restores N(0,1)" (id 25): refuted (exact DFT reproduces the scaling; the cut works only at nf = 2n uniform). Severity high to medium.
- NUFFT-LRT BJD "halves the SNR" (ids 48, 55): dataset-specific; in the verifier's data the wrong statistic was inflated (8.06 to 14.28). The general statement is "not the same statistic" (corr ~0.5). "Keep the fold in float64" (id 26) is not load-bearing: epoch subtraction before the float32 cast suffices.
- Sequential detector "real Kepler/TESS CBVs are not zero-mean" (id 27): not checked against CBV files; the verified condition is any column with |mean|/std >~ depth.
- Detector A fix "restores the Detector A advantage" (id 28's implication): with the residual PSD Detector A only matches the sequential baseline (SNR 8.89 vs 8.89; completeness 0.47/1.00 vs 0.47/1.00); the oracle-PSD gain (11.7) is optimistic.
- "Non-uniform grids need use_fft=False" (id 36): the direct-sum kernel also assumes fmin + i df; only `python_dir_sums=True` (CPU) evaluates the actual grid.
- CE "1-element OOB write on every default run" (id 20): only when the brightest point falls in the last phase bin at the last frequency (roughly 1/phase_bins of runs) and never for use_fast; the in-bounds cross-phase/cross-frequency spill is what happens every run.
- Weighted CE "drop the truncation entirely" (id 21 option 2): unsafe without a pmn floor (inf at 3/3000 frequencies at max_phi = 1e6).
- CE double+fast "runs 12 bytes past its dynamic allocation" (id 22): arithmetically right, by inspection only; memcheck flagged the misalignment, not an overrun.
- `LombScargleAsyncProcess.preallocate` "did not show stale reads" (id 34 aside): it does, 14/30.
- "Keplerian grids with N < 5 kill the context" (id 35): only with `use_fast=True`; default `eebls_transit` raises `qmin/qmax must be finite`. The LS "corr 0.007 for dy = 1e-9" sub-claim compares against a point-dropped reference, which is the wrong reference for a legitimately dominant weight.
- TLS FAP "14 s for 400 noise LCs" (id 10): 1.5 s on the 4090 warm; immaterial.
- `eebls_gpu` "with default max_memory the whole grid is one batch" (id 39): only on large-memory cards.
- Negative performance results already established by the auditors (do not retry): 64-bit packed (yw, w) CAS histogram is 5x slower at TESS/HAT scale (id 138); sigma = 2 is not free speed and m < 8 buys nothing at survey nf; `ndimage.median_filter` is not faster than `medfilt` (id 154); PDM `_fast` kernels are not faster on Ada (ids 114, 159); the "multipass vs fused" discrepancy at T = 3650 d in the bls-core exp1 log was a test artefact (dphi = 1e-9 shifts exact-edge phases), superseded by exp9b (fused == max of the passes to 1e-8).

## 3. Numerical and statistical quality

### 3.1 Precision

- Absolute (BJD-scale) times. BLS (all variants), TLS, Lomb-Scargle, CE and PDM reproduce their t ~ 0..100 results at t + 2457000.5 and t + 2460000.0 to <= 1e-6 relative (most bit-exact), because BLS/TLS subtract floor(min t) in float64 (`utils.subtract_epoch`) and LS/CE/PDM mean-subtract on the host. The two exceptions are confirmed defects: `NUFFTLRTAsyncProcess` (all three detectors; corr 0.48-0.50 with epochs shifted consistently, corr -0.1 without) and `NFFTAsyncProcess` (|ghat| rel 0.94), both from float32 casts of absolute t (spacing 0.25 d at 2.457e6). Both are fixed by float64 epoch subtraction before the cast (verified rel 4e-4 and 1.9e-6). float32 inputs supplied by the caller at BJD scale destroy every method (corr 0.58-0.93; id 131) and should trigger a warning.
- float32 phase folds at large T*f. The compiled BLS fold `mod1(t*f)` is bit-identical to numpy float32; its resolution is ulp(T*f) (1.95e-3 cycles at T*f = 23,019; 1977 distinct phases of 5000 points). Consequences (auditor-measured): q = 0.01 boxes recover 0.968/0.924/0.901 of the float64 power at T*f = 7000/18,250/58,400 (worst 0.846), -6% at 1 yr and -22% at 10 yr for f = 20 c/d (ids 65, 158); Keplerian-q0 boxes are unaffected; sparse BLS at 3650 d x 4.7 c/d loses up to 30% at f_inj and is sort-order dependent at float32 ties (26% at one frequency; id 73); PDM and CE argmax move at 3650 d x 50 c/d (PDM 1056 to 759 at 50 bins; CE 1941 to 1837; id 109). TLS already ships a hi/lo split fold with `fmaf` residual (`tls_fast.cu:231-232`); porting it to `bls_common.cuh`, `bls_batch.cu`, `sparse_bls.cu`, `pdm.cu` is the fix; until then the docs should state the ulp(T*f_max) vs q_min/noverlap criterion.
- Bin alignment. Binned BLS/TLS results move by up to 10% (BLS, noverlap = 2; 11%/9%/7.6% at noverlap 1/4/8) and 2% (TLS chi2) with the fractional part of min(t), with argmax changes on `eebls_transit` and TLS at t + 0.3333 (id 130); t + 2457000.5 equals t + 0.5 to 1e-8, so this is discretization, not precision. Sparse BLS is invariant to 4.7e-4.
- Accumulation. The dense BLS path centres y in float64 before the float32 cast; the sparse GPU kernels do not (confirmed defect 8: 1e-2 relative on mag-12 data, catastrophic with one dominant weight). LS host reductions use Python builtins (slow, numerically fine). NUFFT-LRT host float32 accumulation is fine (numpy pairwise, <= 4e-6 at nf = 1e6).
- `use_double`. LS/NFFT `use_double=True` is currently LESS accurate than float32 at survey scale (confirmed: NFFT 4.2e-2 vs 1.0e-2 rel ||y||_1 at ng = 1.8e6; LS 1.46e-2 vs 1.2e-3 vs astropy) because of `floorf` on a double; after the one-word fix it reaches 1.2e-9..2.5e-9 and 2.4e-3 (the remainder is the psi/k0 defects). cufinufft rejects `use_double` (TypeError; id 97). PDM has no double option. CE `use_double` + `use_fast` crashes for odd (mag_bins + 1)*phase_bins (confirmed). NUFFT-LRT `use_double` is BJD-invariant (5e-9) but does not fix the aliased upper band (algorithmic); sigma = 4 does.
- float32 floor of the fixed LS path (auditor-measured): 1.7e-4 absolute power at f*T ~ 7e3, 2.6e-3 at f*T ~ 2e5, from float32 t storage and un-reduced phases in `nfft_shift`/`normalize`/`lomb.cu` (ids 98, 160); direct sums float32 8e-4 at T = 3650 d. Reducing the phases mod one cycle and recommending `use_double` for FAP-grade work at large f*T is the recommended docs/code follow-up.
- Run-to-run determinism. BLS fast/batch differ by 1e-8..1e-7 (float atomics), TLS chi2 by 4.6e-5 absolute (SDE/period/t0 stable), `eebls_gpu` solutions flip at exact power ties (7-12 of 800 per run), NUFFT-LRT upper band jitters 0.5% (max 0.045 SNR units at n = 18,719); CE, PDM, single-LC LS and sparse BLS are bitwise reproducible. None of this is documented (id 134).

### 3.2 Statistics

- BLS. Power is 1 - chi2/chi2_0 (Kovacs et al. 2002) with normalized weights; `snr` equals astropy `objective='snr'` and `loglik` equals astropy's likelihood x (1 - r) exactly as documented (verified). The fast-path box grid excludes qmax and its phase step (qmin/noverlap) can cost 15-50% of exact power near qmin (id 64, unverified). Sparse BLS reports q biased low by ~1/(2N) relative to the paper's midpoint definition (id 74) and has no minimum in-transit-point floor (single-point "transits" at 30% of trial frequencies in N = 100 noise; id 78).
- TLS. SDE = (max SR - mean SR)/std SR with SR = 1 - chi2/max(chi2), not the reference's chi2_min/chi2: identical under the null (corr 1.000) but 2x lower for strong signals (21.8 vs 45.4 at SNR ~ 110; ids 81, 146), so published SDE thresholds do not transfer. SNR uses max(chi2) over the grid and the coarse chi2 (id 85). The returned FAP is uncalibrated (confirmed: null SDE mean 6.43, std 1.00 at 60 d/6157 periods; 23.3% of noise above 7; 92% above 7 at 365 d/43,780 periods) and the null depends on grid and baseline, so only a per-configuration bootstrap can be honest. `medfilt` zero-padding inflates edge power (id 83); a 0.5-sigma normalization offset halves or doubles SDE because there is no free baseline term (id 80, shared with the reference).
- Lomb-Scargle. `fap_baluev` matches astropy and a 50-digit mpmath Baluev to < 1e-13 (verified), but `batched_run_const_nfreq(only_return_best_freqs=True)` returns `1 - FAP` (exactly 1.0 for any real peak, defeating the issue-#14 log-space rewrite) and uses d_K = 3 for multiharmonic runs (ids 96, 129, 144). After the NFFT fixes a 1e-2 power error near z ~ 0.3, N = 300 is still a ~2x FAP error (d ln FAP/dz ~ -N/2), so FAP-grade work should use `use_double`.
- PDM. Returns 1 - SS_within/SS_total with no degrees-of-freedom correction: pure-noise mean (M - 1)/(N - 1) (0.40 at N = 20, 0.185 at N = 50, 0.009 at N = 1000) whereas the docs and notebook quote Stellingwerf's (N - M)/(N - 1) formulas and "Theta ~ 1 for noise" (id 110). Values are not comparable across nbins, and for gappy data M (occupied bins) varies with frequency. Document, and optionally add `dof_correction`.
- CE. Returns Graham's H(m|phi) + log((mag_overlap + 1)/mag_bins) (measured offset -1.6069 vs log(1/5) = -1.6094, the residual being the misbinning); `compute_log_prob` is the Poisson log-likelihood under the phase-independent null and is minimized at the true frequency (undocumented; id 116). The confirmed misbinning bias is O(1/N) on every run and the weighted histogram loses 9% of the mass on average.
- NUFFT-LRT. The statistic is a whitened correlation whose null scale is not 1: with the TRUE white PSD the Monte-Carlo null std is 0.97/1.38/1.95 (uniform, nf = n/2n/4n) and 2.16/2.65/3.38 (ground sampling), 3.06 with an oracle red-noise PSD, and the campaign's own calibration measured 1.812 (estimated PSD, nf = 2n). The verifier reproduced the ground-sampling value with an exact float64 DFT (2.455 vs NFFT 2.441), so it is intrinsic to frequency-diagonal whitening under a spectral window (non-orthogonal modes; exact aliases for uniform nf > n), not NFFT error and not PSD estimation as `docs/NUFFT_LRT_README.md:116-121` says. Self-whitening costs 24-28% of SNR at the detection threshold (id 120). Detector A's frequency-domain statistic is a constant ~1.5x multiple of the exact time-domain statistic (Gram overcount 2.2-2.4x, id 143), so rankings are preserved but the prior is effectively ~2.3x wider than specified. Detection thresholds must be calibrated empirically per configuration, as the harness does.

## 6. NUFFT-LRT release readiness

### 6.1 The campaign (scripts/nufft_lrt_validation.py, run on the pod 2026-09-03/04, seed 20260711)

Protocol: 600-point ground-based sampling over 90 d (nightly windows, 35% weather loss), 32 log-spaced trial periods in 2-18 d with the true period 5.3 d placed on the grid, durations {0.12, 0.25} d, box transits of duration 0.22 d at random epochs, formal errors sigma_white = 3e-3. Per configuration: 60 signal-free light curves give each arm's 95th-percentile null maximum (its 5% per-search false-alarm threshold), then 60 injections per depth; a detection requires statistic > threshold AND best period within 1% of P, 2P or P/2. Noise: white; white + OU red with sigma_red = 1x or 3x sigma_white (tau = 0.8 d); and white + 1x red + three shared systematics modes (6/3/6 sigma) with a PCA basis and coefficient prior estimated from a 60-LC population. Arms: `lrt` (whitened matched filter, PSD estimated from the data, epoch grid of round(2P/0.12) clipped to 8..96 per period, 4982 templates per search), `bls` (`eebls_gpu_fast`, q in [0.005, 0.08]), `tls` (`tls_search_batch`, same q window, SDE), `lrt_flat` (PSD = ones, red_3x only), `lrt_marg` (Detector A, default `estimate_psd`), `lrt_seq` (OLS cotrend + matched filter). Wall time: white 4392 s, red_1x 2361 s, red_3x 4715 s, red_sys 7140 s (5.2 h total; ids 29/54/62 attribute ~90% of the LRT GPU time to per-template allocation).

Resolution caveat: with 60 nulls the p95 threshold is the third-highest null maximum; with 60 injections completeness has granularity 0.017 and a binomial 1-sigma of ~0.06 at p ~ 0.5. Differences below ~0.1 are not significant.

Calibration line: "LRT SNR calibration on white noise: mean = 0.007 std = 1.812 (want ~0, ~1)" (200 draws at one fixed template, `nf = 2n`, estimated PSD). The harness's own expectation fails.

What the numbers say:

- White noise: LRT and BLS are indistinguishable (0.750 vs 0.833 at depth 0.004, 0.167 vs 0.133 at 0.002; both within 1.5 sigma). Both beat the TLS arm by 0.27-0.35 at 0.004.
- Red noise 1x and 3x: LRT and BLS are again indistinguishable (0.517 vs 0.467 at 0.008 in red_1x; 0.133 vs 0.100 and 0.933 vs 0.883 in red_3x). The `lrt_flat` arm, which removes the PSD whitening, is at least as complete as the whitened arm (0.217 vs 0.133 at 0.016; 0.950 vs 0.933 at 0.032). So the campaign shows no measurable benefit from PSD whitening at these sample sizes, consistent with the auditor's 10-trial proxy (id 6 of the nufft-lrt audit) and with the 24-28% self-whitening loss (id 120). The LRT's null thresholds rise with red noise (8.4, 10.6, 12.4) exactly as BLS's do (0.035, 0.120, 0.186): the whitening does not stabilize the null.
- Shared systematics: every basis-free arm collapses (LRT 0.30, BLS 0.15, TLS 0.017 at depth 0.032). The gain comes from the cotrending basis, not from whitening: `lrt_seq` (OLS projection then matched filter) recovers 0.567/0.950/1.000 at 0.008/0.016/0.032. Detector A (`lrt_marg`, the README-recommended detector) reaches only 0.200/0.600/1.000, 0.37 and 0.35 below the sequential baseline it is meant to supersede. This is the confirmed `lrt-detectorA-defeated` defect: the verifier reproduced the same ordering with the harness's own data model (default marginal 0.13/0.57 vs 0.47/1.00 with a residual-based PSD vs 0.47/1.00 sequential at depths 0.008/0.016), so the lrt_marg row measures the PSD-contamination bug, not Detector A.
- The TLS arm: the injected signal is a box, which matches the BLS and LRT templates exactly and mismatches TLS's limb-darkened template by construction; the TLS statistic here is an SDE computed from a 32-point spectrum (by inspection of the harness). The TLS numbers should not be read as a general TLS-vs-BLS statement.
- Coverage of the confirmed defects by the harness: t lies in [0, 90] (the BJD cast is not exercised), every arm passes an explicit epoch grid (`epochs=None` is not exercised), and the PCA modes are exactly zero-mean because the population is row-centred (`scripts/nufft_lrt_validation.py:197`; the sequential-intercept defect is not exercised). The default sigma = 2 upper band IS exercised by every LRT arm (verifier: z-scores within 2%, ranking unchanged), and the Detector A PSD defect IS exercised by lrt_marg. So the campaign is valid for what it ran, but it does not validate the default public path.

### 6.2 Confirmed defects on the module (details in Section 2)

1. BJD float32 cast (blocker, ids 26/30/48/55): all three detectors return a different statistic for absolute times (corr 0.48-0.50; sequential best period 4.4 to 6.4 d); epoch subtraction before the cast restores rel <= 4e-4.
2. `epochs=None` (blocker, id 24): the documented default is a single phase-0 template; 0/12 recovery of random-epoch transits vs 12/12 with an epoch grid; the shipped example's "detection" is at SNR 0.64 at the true cell.
3. Sequential intercept (high, ids 27/58): OLS without centring; a 1% column mean drops SNR@P from 25.0 to 5.4 and moves the best period; only the zero-mean campaign basis hides it.
4. Upper half band (medium, id 25): modes k >= nf/2 carry O(1) aliasing error at the default sigma = 2 (also in double; float32 non-deterministic); sigma = 4 or a 2 nf transform fixes it; z-scores change < 2%.
5. Detector A vs `estimate_psd` (high, id 28; 142/157): the default PSD includes the realized systematics (median inflation 36x across the band) and whitens the transit away; 3.8-9x SNR loss, completeness 0.13 vs 0.47 at depth 0.008; the fix (PSD from the basis-projected residual) restores parity with the sequential baseline, not superiority.
   Also unverified but relevant: `NFFTAsyncProcess.run(memory=)` returns unsynchronized, un-zeroed results (ids 118/155; blocks the reuse speedup), zero PSD bins give 1e6/nan (id 121), singular priors become flat priors (ids 122/156), the PSD convention and return shape are undocumented (id 123), the README's validation section is an empty placeholder pointing at a directory that does not exist (id 123), and the module ignores `dy`.

### 6.3 The null-std 1.8 calibration issue

The harness expects the statistic at a fixed template on white noise to be N(0, 1) and measures std 1.812. The auditors' Monte-Carlo shows the same at the true white PSD: uniform sampling 0.97/1.38/1.95 for nf = n/2n/4n, ground sampling 2.16/2.65/3.38, oracle red-noise PSD 3.06; the verifier reproduced the ground value with an exact float64 DFT (2.455 vs 2.441 on device) and showed that cutting the band to k < nf/2 only moves it to 2.03 for ground sampling. The over-dispersion is therefore intrinsic to summing frequency-diagonal whitened products over non-orthogonal NFFT modes of irregularly sampled data (and over exact aliases for uniform nf > n); it is not an NFFT accuracy defect and is not explained by PSD estimation as `docs/NUFFT_LRT_README.md:116-121` claims. Two consequences: the raw value is not an SNR and grows with nf (28.6 to 51.3 for nf = n to 8n at a fixed resolved template), so the README's "raise nf for short transits" advice inflates numbers without adding information; and any threshold must be calibrated empirically per (sampling, nf, PSD estimator) configuration, exactly as the harness does with null p95. The only way to get an N(0, 1) statistic by construction is the exact time-domain GLS form (C from the estimated PSD; id 119, medium effort, n up to a few thousand).

### 6.4 Recommendation

The method is not ready to be released as an official, non-experimental method in 1.0. Its default public path (`run(t, y, periods, durations)` on BJD-scale times) fails twice over (wrong epoch handling, wrong time handling), the recommended Detector A is beaten by its own baseline because of a PSD-estimation defect, the shipped example and README present a non-search as a detection, and the campaign (the only injection-recovery evidence) sidesteps three of the five defects and shows no gain over BLS in white or red noise. None of this is hard to fix; all of it must be fixed and re-measured before the label changes.

Before it can be released as official:

1. Fix and test: float64 epoch subtraction in `run()` (+ BJD invariance test); automatic epoch grid for `epochs=None` (+ nonzero-epoch test) or refuse None; centre V and y in `_sequential_detrend` (+ non-zero-mean basis test); PSD from the basis-projected residual for `detector='marginal'`; sigma = 4 default or 2 nf transform (+ full-band NFFT test); zero and synchronize the NFFT buffer on the reuse path; floor user PSDs and validate their length; PSD-safe prior inversion with a positive-definite check; validate inputs (finite t/y, N >= 3, warn that dy is ignored); then take the per-run NFFT memory reuse (LRT-1) so the re-validation is affordable.
2. Re-validate with the fixed code: re-run all four configurations and all arms, with at least 200 injections per depth if the 0.05-level LRT-vs-BLS and whitened-vs-flat differences are to be resolved; add a config with t offset by +2457000.5, an arm that uses `epochs=None` (the public default), and a config with a non-zero-mean basis; keep the null-p95 calibration; archive the JSON under `benchmarks/results/` and fill the README placeholder from it (`scripts/summarize_lrt_validation.py`). Only then quote Detector A numbers.
3. Rewrite the documentation. The honest text, based on the evidence in hand: "The whitened NUFFT matched filter matched BLS's completeness in white noise and in OU red noise at 1x and 3x the white level (differences <= 0.08 at 60 injections per depth) and showed no measurable gain over a flat-PSD matched filter; PSD whitening does not stabilize the false-alarm threshold (null p95 8.4 to 12.4 with red noise, as for BLS). With a shared-systematics basis the sequential cotrend + matched filter recovered 0.57/0.95/1.00 of transits at depths 0.008/0.016/0.032 where BLS and TLS without a basis recovered 0.00/0.05/0.15 and 0.00/0.00/0.02; Detector A results are pending re-measurement after the PSD fix. The statistic is a whitened correlation, not an SNR: its null standard deviation is 1.8-2.6 for ground-based sampling even with the true PSD, grows with nf, and must be calibrated empirically per configuration (the validation script shows how). Times are epoch-subtracted internally; `epochs=None` scans an automatic epoch grid at a cost of ~2P/duration transforms per (period, duration)." State the PSD convention with a formula, the return shapes, and that `dy` is not used.
4. If the fixes and re-validation cannot land before the 1.0 tag, keep the module import-warned EXPERIMENTAL, remove it from the release-notes headline list, drop the class-docstring/README/example presentations of `epochs=None` as a search, and add the BJD caveat, so that 1.0 ships no default path that is silently wrong. The Detector A and sequential arms were never audited before this review and should carry the same warning until their tests exist.
