# TLS component evidence

The report, tables, figures, per-job JSON results/logs, source snapshots and original verification receipt are retained here. The original full snapshot is recoverable at [commit f0dc981](https://github.com/johnh2o2/cuvarbase/tree/f0dc981/analysis/tls-profile-20260908). Its manifests refer to the original paths.
